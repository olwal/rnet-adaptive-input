# Hardware and pinout

Identifying the joystick, the verified 9-pin D-sub pinout, the measurements it was verified against, and the bring-up procedure.

## Hardware

**Joystick** (user-supplied, accessibility/mobility salvage):
- Permobil R-Net specialty joystick
- Labels: `ART 0035-7003a`, `PAB 1822988`
- References `SK78813` (which is actually the R-Net Omni *technical manual* number — indicates this joystick was sold as an Omni-compatible SID input device)
- Connector: 9-pin D-sub male (cable end)
- Auxiliary: 3.5mm mono jack on joystick body (for external user switch / "buddy button")

**Goal**: read the joystick from a generic microcontroller (Teensy 3.0; HID gamepad or other applications later). No R-Net Omni controller in the loop. The Teensy's native USB also makes the HID stretch goal straightforward — no ATmega32U4 needed.

## Confirmed pinout (9-pin D-sub)

Verified by combination of: PG Drives R-Net Omni technical manual, Bob Paradiso's CustomSID KiCad schematic, and direct DMM measurements on the user's specific unit.

| Pin | Function | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Y-axis** analog out (SPEED, fwd/rev) | ~5.6 V at rest, swings 4.5–6.8 V (±1.15 V) at full deflection. 1.8 kΩ series resistor inside joystick. |
| **2** | **X-axis** analog out (DIR, left/right) | Same characteristics as pin 1. 1.8 kΩ series resistor inside joystick. |
| **3** | **Vref** out | Rock-solid 5.6 V at this unit's supply voltage. Generated by the joystick, NOT supplied by the host. |
| 4 | unused | Bob's schematic shows NoConn. |
| 5 | Detect (optional) | PG manual says internally tied to 0V inside the joystick. Bob's design ignores it; appears not required by this unit. |
| **6** | **MODE switch input** | Connects internally to the 3.5mm jack tip. Open by default, shorts to GND when switch pressed. |
| **7** | **+12V supply IN** | Joystick draws ~30 mA at idle with LED on. |
| **8** | **GND (0V)** | Tied to D-sub shell. |
| 9 | unused | PG Omni2 manual says paralleled with pin 7 (+12V) on the controller side, but the joystick doesn't require it. |

### Pin numbering reference (male connector, viewed from PIN side, pins toward you)

```
        1   2   3   4   5
         •   •   •   •   •
           •   •   •   •
           6   7   8   9
```

Pin 1 = upper-left. Pin 5 = upper-right. Pin 6 = lower-left. Pin 9 = lower-right. *Mirror-reverse if probing from the back/solder side.*

### Measurement evidence (joystick UNPOWERED)

| Test | Value | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Pin 7 ↔ pin 8 | 30 kΩ | Input rail to GND through internal regulator + filter caps. Not a short, not open — normal. Reading creeps upward as the DMM charges the input caps, so treat it as approximate; the point is that it is neither ~0 Ω nor open. |
| Pin 3 ↔ pin 8 | ~0.9 MΩ | Op-amp output to GND when unpowered — normal. |
| Pin 1 ↔ pin 8 | ~96.4 kΩ static | Static buffer output network; doesn't change with stick movement until powered. Normal. |

### Measurement evidence (joystick POWERED, ~11V at pin 7)

| Test | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pin 7 (joystick side, after 33 Ω series) | ~11 V | Joystick draws ~30 mA, 33 Ω drops ~1 V. |
| Pin 3 (Vref) | **5.6 V steady** | Matches the center of pin 1/2 swing. |
| Pin 1 with stick movement | **4.5 V to 6.8 V** | ±1.15 V around 5.6 V center, matches Bob's spec. |
| Pin 2 with stick movement | **4.5 V to 6.8 V** | Symmetric to pin 1. |
| LED | Solid **green** | Joystick is happy; no fault. |

## Bring-up procedure (already completed — for reference / repeat)

1. **DMM-only sanity checks (joystick unpowered):**
   - Confirm pin 7 ↔ pin 8 is a moderate resistance (kΩ range), not a short or open.
   - Confirm pin 8 ↔ shell is near 0 Ω (shell is GND).
   - Confirm pin 3 ↔ pin 8 reads high but finite resistance.

2. **First power-up (current-limited):**
   - 12 V supply with ~150 mA current limit, OR a 100 Ω resistor in series with pin 7 as a "soft fuse".
   - Initial 100 Ω caused brown-out (joystick draws 30 mA, drops 3 V across 100 Ω, joystick gets only ~9 V — regulator marginal). **Symptom**: pin 7 voltage bouncing 8.5–9 V, joystick LED blinking irregularly.
   - **Fix**: reduce series resistor to **33 Ω** (or short it after verifying no fault). At 33 Ω, joystick sees ~11 V, runs cleanly, LED solid green.

3. **Voltage probing (with joystick running):**
   - Pin 3 should be a stable ~6 V (5.5–6.0 V depending on supply).
   - Pins 1 and 2 should track pin 3 at rest, swing ~±1.2 V at full deflection.
   - Push stick forward/back to identify Y-axis (pin 1); left/right for X-axis (pin 2).
